# Step clips worth making

A shortlist of the steps in `constants/protocols.ts` where a demonstration clip teaches something the three lines of text cannot: hand position, treat placement, leash shape, timing, or the dog's body language. Step numbers are zero-based indexes, as in `constants/stepMedia.ts`.

**41 unique clips cover 77 steps**, because several courses share the same mechanic (the sit lure, the stay palm, the closed fist). `STEP_MEDIA` can point many steps at one file. `crate_s1` (5 clips) is already done and not counted.

Rule used to pick: a step gets a clip only if an owner could read the text and still do it wrong with their body. Sniff-break endings, "repeat in a new place", duration ladders, schedule and camera-review steps are all out.

## Courses that get few or no clips, on purpose

- **Separation anxiety**: the steps are the owner's routine (keys, shoes, leaving, watching the camera). Nothing in the dog's body to demonstrate; a clip adds nothing over the text.
- **Stage 3 of most courses**: these repeat a stage 1 or 2 mechanic with a helper or in a new place. They can reuse the earlier clip or go without.
- **Every closing sniff or play step.**

## Rough cost and size

- 41 clips at the ~$0.28 pipeline is about $12 if every one lands first try; budget $25 to $35 for retries.
- The five crate clips total ~9 MB. 41 more at that rate is ~75 MB, too much to bundle. Either re-encode smaller (5 s of flat 2D art at 768p should compress to well under 1 MB) or host them and download per course.
- A clip currently replaces the rep counter. 32 of the 44 Tier 1 steps are rep steps, so the layout needs to fit both before this ships widely.

## Accuracy checks before any clip ships

The details that matter most are the ones a generator gets wrong: treat at the hip not in front (5, 9), treat from the *other* hand (13, 24), hand frozen not yanked (17), leash visibly slack in a J (7, 8), the distant dog genuinely far away (19, 20). A clip that shows the mistake is worse than no clip.
