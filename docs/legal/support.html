Support

Pawly is a daily dog-training app. This page covers how to reach us and the most common account and billing questions.

Contact us

Email support@pawly.app. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the app version shown in your message if you write from the app (Profile, then Contact support).

Pawly can't help in an emergency. If your dog is hurt or unwell, call your vet.

Cancel or change a subscription

Pawly Pro is billed by Apple, so you manage it with Apple, not in Pawly.

  1. On your iPhone, open the App Store.
  2. Tap your picture at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Pawly, then Cancel subscription or pick a different plan.

You can also open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, or in Pawly go to Profile and choose Manage subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. If you started a free trial, it becomes a paid subscription when it ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then. Deleting the app or your Pawly account does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves because we never receive your payment details.

Restore purchases

If you paid but the app still shows the free tier, or you have a new phone or a fresh install:

  1. Log in to the same Pawly account you used when you subscribed.
  2. Open Profile and tap Pawly Pro.
  3. Choose Restore purchases.

Make sure the App Store is using the Apple Account you bought with. If Pro still does not turn on, email us with the date of the purchase.

Delete your account

  1. Open Pawly and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your dog's profile and avatar, plans, session and walk logs, and coach conversations. It cannot be undone. Cancel Pawly Pro with Apple first, because deleting your account does not cancel the subscription.

If you can't get into the app, email support@pawly.app from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

The live coach and your camera

While a live coach session is running, Pawly sends still frames from the camera to our AI provider to count reps and give feedback. Pawly does not save the frames and does not record audio. You can always train manually without the camera. The privacy policy has the details.

When to see a professional

Pawly's coach is AI and can make mistakes. It isn't a substitute for a vet or certified behaviorist. Get hands-on help if your dog has bitten or tried to bite, if anyone has been injured, if behavior changes suddenly (see your vet first), or if your dog panics when left alone.
